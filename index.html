About Me

Hey there! I'm Rohan, a tech enthusiast from Colorado who's really into AI, robotics, and cloud tech. At Georgia Tech, I've shaped my studies to dive into hands-on, practical experiences in these areas. When I'm not busy building or exploring, you'll probably find me cheering on the Denver Nuggets or nerding out about the newest tech trends—I like staying in the loop.

Feel free to hit me up to chat about tech, robotics, the outdoors, or even just basketball!
