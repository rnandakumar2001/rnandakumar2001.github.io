About Me

Hey! I’m Rohan, a Computer Science graduate from Georgia Tech with a strong interest in cloud infrastructure, AI, and robotics. I chose the Artificial Intelligence and Devices threads to dive deep into both intelligent systems and how they interact with the real world. I’ve had the chance to turn those interests into hands-on experience, including a Software Development Engineering internship at Amazon Web Services, where I worked on building secure, scalable cloud services. That experience shaped how I think about production systems, reliability, and designing software that actually gets used. I enjoy exploring a wide range of technologies from reinforcement learning and robotics to web and backend development and figuring out how they fit together in practical ways. This site is a collection of some of the projects I’ve built, either solo or with friends, as I explore different problem spaces and grow as an engineer. Hope you enjoy looking around.
